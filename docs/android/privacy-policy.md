TypeMate is a private, on-device dictation app by CodeWalnut. This
policy explains what data TypeMate handles and what never leaves your
device.

## The short version

Your voice and your words stay on your device. TypeMate has no account
system, no advertising, and no cloud transcription.

## Microphone audio

TypeMate records audio only while you actively hold the dictation
button. Recordings are transcribed on your device by a locally stored
speech model and are deleted immediately after transcription. Audio is
never uploaded anywhere. If a transcription fails, the recording is
kept locally only so you can retry it, and it is deleted on retry,
history clear, or after 30 days.

## Transcripts and history

Your dictation history is stored only on your device, in the app's
private storage. Deleting the app deletes it. TypeMate cannot read,
access, or transmit your history.

## Speech model download

On first use, TypeMate downloads its speech recognition model files
over the internet (about 640 MB). This is a plain file download; no
personal data is sent with it beyond what any internet download
involves (your IP address, as seen by the file host).

## Optional anonymous error reports

By default, TypeMate sends nothing. If you turn on "Share anonymous
error reports" in Settings, technical crash and error data (error
messages, app version, device model, OS version) is sent to Sentry, our
error-reporting processor, to help us fix bugs. These reports never
include audio, transcripts, or your dictation history. You can turn
this off at any time in Settings.

## Permissions

- **Microphone:** to record your dictation while you hold the button.
- **Internet:** to download the speech model once, and to send optional
  anonymous error reports if you enable them.

## Children

TypeMate is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal
data from anyone.
